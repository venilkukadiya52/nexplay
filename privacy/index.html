Privacy.

NEXPLAY stores favorites and motion preferences in your own browser using localStorage. It does not operate user accounts or collect analytics. External game websites have their own privacy policies. We do not run advertising or analytics. External game sites and the third-party 3D library provider may receive technical connection data (such as IP address) when your browser accesses their servers. Your device stores favorites and display preferences locally; you can clear these in browser settings. Contact the site owner through the GitHub repository where you found this beta.
